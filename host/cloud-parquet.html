Note 02 · Cloud

The files went to a bucket

16 July 2026AmsterdamJordi Lluis

Reconstructed 6 September 2026 from the metals-paper status report and Pattern Forge’s public catalog.

After live Polymarket 5-minute trading stopped, the work moved to recordings. On this date a paper-only pipeline was in Google Cloud, region europe-west1: a state bucket, Cloud Run jobs, hourly and daily schedules. No broker credentials. Execution enabled: false.

What was stored, and what Pattern Forge later ships
Venue in the filesPolymarket Perps hourly candles, not CME spot, not a cash index.
Status snapshotWTI 1,624 hourly rows in that day’s venue table, with 2,414,184 total trades on the selected contract — venue stats, not my fill count.
Public catalog nowTen parquet series copied from Google Cloud Storage into Pattern Forge. 20,567 closed hourly bars after omitting a possibly still-forming last bar. Gaps are kept, not interpolated.
WTI in the catalog2,482 closed bars, 42 missing hours, SHA-256 6733ae8ec1646133…

A data point here is a closed hour: open, high, low, close, timestamp. It is not a forecast. Models on that day’s paper ledger promoted zero venue-native candidates. Candidate net return was not positive.

Conclusion I will defend: I can put market files in a bucket, hash them, and refuse to fill holes. That is not a live Hyperliquid bot, and it is not a warehouse of official energy statistics.
