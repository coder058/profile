The switch after live Polymarket trading was not “I now trade Hyperliquid.” It was: public perpetual data, a chart workspace, and a research harness that is allowed to reject its own ideas.

A private Hyperliquid research tree separately records a live book recorder (one connection, hundreds of subscriptions) and a large historical bar store. Its own summary is that tested retail-scale strategies did not clear spread. I am not republishing that as a product, and I am not claiming a Hyperliquid desk.

Conclusion I will defend: Hyperliquid in the portfolio is market data I can fetch, cache, replay without lookahead, and falsify against. It is not a second live bot.
