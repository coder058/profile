Hyperliquid is a data venue

01 August 2026AmsterdamJordi Lluis

After live Polymarket trading I did not “move the desk to Hyperliquid.” I used public perpetual data: a chart workspace, and a research harness that is allowed to reject its own ideas.

What Hyperliquid is in the public work
Live midPublic WebSocket allMids. Heartbeat, reconnect, no trading account.
Closed candlesFixed public candleSnapshot endpoint, validated in the Next.js API.
Bundled BTC file5,000 contiguous 5-minute mainnet candles, 11–28 July 2026. A scenario bank, not a performance report.
1 August sweepsVPIN, vol-state momentum, oscillator divergence, vol-spike exits: closed as alpha or harmful.

A private tree also has a live book recorder and a large historical bar store. Its own summary is that tested retail-scale rules did not clear the spread. I am not republishing that as a product.

In the portfolio, Hyperliquid is data I can fetch, cache, replay without look-ahead, and throw away. It is not a second live bot.
