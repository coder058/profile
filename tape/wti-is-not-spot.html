“WTI” is not spot

06 September 2026AmsterdamJordi Lluis

If you open Pattern Forge and pick WTI Oil, you are not looking at Cushing cash or a CME settlement. You are looking at an hourly Polymarket Perps recording I copied into the app.

I read the numbers below from the committed JSON on 6 September 2026. I did not rebuild the parquet, and I did not splice a live oil feed on top.

Replay only uses candles up to the cursor. Later prices stay out of the indicators. Incomplete higher-timeframe groups are dropped. The public demo has no PostgreSQL; storage in CI is a different path, not this file’s venue.

This is a perpetual-contract tape with holes. It is not a forecast, and it is not physical oil.
