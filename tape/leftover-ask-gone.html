The leftover ask was gone

06 August 2026AmsterdamJordi Lluis

Reconstructed 6 September 2026 from research_lottery/VERDICT.md, dated 6 August 2026.

This is the note about whether the old leftover-cheap-ask condition still existed. I am using its counts. I am not using its headline that a patch killed a proven edge.

Later-book scan used in the 6 August note
Markets12,929 five-minute BTC/ETH/SOL markets, 26 June–10 July, fixed time-to-expiry checkpoints.
Full stream104.5 million BBO updates across about 29 days.
Stale-ask triggerWinner ask ≤ $0.30 while the loser looked decided (bid ≤ 0.05): one occurrence in that stream. Apparent both-sides crosses: persistence p50 0.00s, max 0.02s. None lasted a second.
MissingNo order sizes in the early BBO recorder. August files gone (the VPS was unreachable). That is a hole, not a hidden win.

The later book looked tight and two-sided at those checkpoints. That supports a changed environment after the April–May live window. It does not isolate CLOB V2, the 2 May guard removal, or competition as the cause. Live trading had already stopped. This is a recording study.

Conclusion I will defend: the leftover cheap ask was not a durable later-market object in these files. “The patch killed the edge” is still an inference I will not publish as fact.
