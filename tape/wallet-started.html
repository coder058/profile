Funding

The wallet started at $19.98

16 April 2026AmsterdamJordi Lluis

I went live on Polymarket this day. For months I said “about $18.” The on-chain path is tighter, and I wrote it up in July.

Observed funding, 16 April 2026
18:01:40 UTC$21.989505 native Polygon USDC entered the wallet.
Before collateral$2.00 went to a contract.
Swap$19.98 native USDC became $19.979317 USDC.e.
Left outside$0.009505 native USDC remained.

The first branch bought late favorites, often $0.96–$0.99, with one to twenty seconds left. Even when the side was right, there was almost nothing left to earn. Later branches were an attempt to stop doing that — not a finished edge.

Live trading started with about twenty dollars of observed collateral. I will not keep writing $18 as if that were the chain.
