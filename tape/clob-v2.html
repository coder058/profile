This is a real platform event. It sits four days before the bot’s own 2 May code edits. Timing overlap is not a controlled experiment. I do not get to say “the patch killed the edge” from the calendar alone.

Conclusion I will defend: 28 April is a venue cutover. 2 May is an internal bot change. Later losses do not assign causality to one of those two dates.
